SEDS at UCSD

Students for the Exploration and Development of Space (SEDS) at UCSD is a student-run collegiate rocketry organization.

I serve as the Lead for the fluids subteam of our bipropellant liquid rocket Riptide.

I am also the Director of Internal Affairs and a member of the executive board!

Riptide at FAR (Friends of Amateur Rocketry)
Testing Site in Mojave Desert

Fluids Lead - Riptide

As Fluids Lead on Riptide I led a team of 7 engineers through the manufacturing, test campaign, and following troubleshooting of our propellant feed system.

I authored and maintained the system P&ID (pictured below!), developed a custom MATLAB script to model major and minor system head losses and verify propellant mass flow requirements.

I wrote procedures and led hands-on leak check efforts that laid the foundational architecture for our subsequent self-landing vehicle development.

Director of Internal Affairs

As a member of the executive board, I directed organization-wide recruitment for SEDS@UCSD, evaluating over 300 applicants (+20% from previous years).

Then I led the selection, onboarding, and mentorship of 30 incoming members across our propulsion, structures, avionics, and controls subteams.

Beyond managing high-volume applicant reviews and organizing recruitment mixers, I served as a key mediator and problem dissipator within the club, navigating interpersonal dynamics, aligning cross-subteam priorities, and resolving technical or operational friction between members to keep complex projects moving forward smoothly.
