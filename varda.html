Varda Space Industries

Vehicle Systems Integration & Test Engineering Intern

During my internship at Varda Space Industries, I authored specialized N2 fill, vent, and leak-check procedures for orbital satellite bus bi-propellant diaphragm tanks, directly mitigating flight hardware risk while enabling safe technician execution.

To close a critical company test-safety gap, I developed an Excel stored energy calculator translating pressure and volume into TNT equivalents per NASA and OSHA standards to establish safe blast radii for all personnel.

Additionally, I provided hands-on support for comprehensive satellite bus verification and validation campaigns, executing vibration testing, mass properties measurements, and sensor checkouts.

Varda was an excellent opportunity to confirm my love for never-ending learning, root-cause analysis, and problem-solving in a fast-paced industry environment! Transitioning from student rocketry to real flight hardware taught me what it really takes to bridge the gap between engineering design and hands-on technician execution on the shop floor. More than anything, this experience solidified that I thrive right in the middle of hardware integration where systems come together, unexpected challenges pop up, and every day demands a new solution.
